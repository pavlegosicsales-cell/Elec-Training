## Services
Full electrician pathway — beginner Level 2 through to ECS JIB Gold Card. Prices as listed on site (ex VAT / inc VAT):

- **Level 2 Diploma in Electrical Installation — C&G 2365-02** — £2,707.50 (£3,249.00 inc VAT). "The first step on the ladder to becoming a fully qualified electrician." For those with little or no experience.
- **Level 3 Diploma in Electrical Installation — C&G 2366-03** — £2,707.50 (£3,249.00 inc VAT). Advanced theory: electrical design, environmental systems, inspection & testing, fault-finding.
- **NVQ Level 3 Electrical Installation/Maintenance — C&G 2357** — £1,874.17 (£2,249.00 inc VAT). Work-based. Can be completed remotely via their online evidence system. Site assessments cost extra.
- **18th Edition Wiring Regulations — C&G 2382-26** — £499.17 (£599.00 inc VAT). Five-day BS 7671 course covering Amendment 4.
- **Inspection & Testing — C&G 2391-52** — £1,079.16 (£1,295.00 inc VAT). Five-day practical, single- and three-phase.
- **Domestic & Commercial EV Charging Installation — C&G 2921-34** — £329.17 (£395.00 inc VAT).
- **ECS JIB Gold Card (full bundled route)** — £8,208.33 (£9,849.99 inc VAT). Theory + practical + NVQ evidence + two on-site assessments, leading to AM2 and Gold Card application.
- **Fast Track Electrician Course** — compressed 6–18 month route vs 3–4 year standard.
- **Guaranteed NVQ work placement + recruitment support** — their flagship differentiator, see below.

Career pathway they sell (use this as a site section — it converts):
Stage 1 Level 2 → 18th Edition → Stage 2 Level 3 → Stage 3 NVQ 2357 (+ on-site assessments) → Stage 4 AM2 (2.5 days, independent centre) → Stage 5 claim NVQ → Apply for ECS Gold Card.

## Target Audience
Adults retraining or changing careers into the electrical trade — typically 25–50, no prior experience, often leaving construction, the military, or unrelated work.
