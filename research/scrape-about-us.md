# About Elec Training

Elec Training is a registered UK electrical training provider based in Wolverhampton, West Midlands. We specialise in City & Guilds-accredited courses for adult learners, including:

– Level 2 Diploma in Electrical Installation (2365-02)

– Level 3 Diploma in Electrical Installation (2366-03)

– NVQ Level 3 in Electrical Installation and Maintenance (2357)

– 18th Edition Wiring Regulations (2382)

– Electric Vehicle Charger Installation (2921)

The centre supports learners from the West Midlands and across the UK. Courses are delivered on-site by qualified t utors with industry experience. Elec Training focuses on hands-on practical learning, career progression, and employer-relevant skills.

The training centre was officially opened in December 2023 by the Mayor of the West Midlands and was featured in the Express & Star. [\[View article\]](https://www.expressandstar.com/business/mayor-opens-training-centre-5835661)
