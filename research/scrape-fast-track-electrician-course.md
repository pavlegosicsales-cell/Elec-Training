Want to become an electrician as quickly as possible? Then you have come to the right page. How can you become fully qualified as quickly as possible?

Here is the most recognised route. City & Guilds [**Level 2 – 2365-02**](https://elec.training/courses/electrical-installation-level-2-city-guilds-2365-02/) 4 weeks in-centre \+ 2 weeks online (6 weeks total).

Next step – City & Guilds [**18th edition course**](https://elec.training/courses/18th-edition-course-city-guilds-2382-26/), 5 days (open book).

At this stage you have two choices,

The workplace competence portfolio, built through real site evidence and assessor sign-off, with 2 on-site assessments included in the £500. This is the stage that turns classroom qualifications into a recognised, employable qualification, and it’s supported by our guaranteed work placement.

The final independent practical exam, taken at an approved centre once your NVQ portfolio is complete.

FAQS

What is the fast track route to becoming an electrician?

You start with City & Guilds Level 2 (2365-02), move onto the 18th Edition course, then Level 3 (2366-03), before progressing to your NVQ Level 3 (2357) and AM2. Once that’s complete, you can apply for your ECS JIB Gold Card.

How quickly can I become a qualified electrician with Elec Training?

You can complete Level 2 and Level 3 back-to-back for the fastest route through the classroom stages, or go into paid work with our recruitment team first and complete Level 3 later while you build experience — whichever suits your circumstances.

Do I need any experience to start?

No. The Fast Track programme is designed for career changers with no prior electrical experience — you don’t need any background in the trade to enrol.

Can I spread the cost of my training?

Yes, 0% finance is available with a £250 deposit and staged payments, so you don’t need to pay for the full course upfront.

What happens after I finish my NVQ 2357?

You’ll need to pass the independent AM2 exam (around £800, arranged at a centre local to you) before you can apply for your ECS JIB Gold Card and work as a fully qualified electrician.

Can I go straight from Level 2 to working, without waiting to finish Level 3?

Yes — many learners go into paid work with our recruitment team after Level 2 and the 18th Edition, building confidence and income while completing Level 3 alongside.

What qualification do I end up with at the end?

Once you’ve completed Levels 2 and 3, the 18th Edition, your NVQ 2357, and passed the AM2, you’re eligible to apply for your ECS JIB Gold Card as a fully approved electrician.
