## Introduction to Your Electrician Career Path in the UK

### Stage 1 - Level 2 Diploma - C&G 2365-02

On your journey to becoming a fully qualified electrician, the level 2 [diploma](https://elec.training/courses/electrical-installation-level-3-2366/) in Electrical Installations is the starting point. This is predominantly the domestic sector and encompasses: Domestic [Wiring](https://elec.training/courses/18th-edition-course-city-guilds-2382-26/), Health and Safety, Installation Theory and Electrical Science.

[Learn More](https://elec.training/courses/electrical-installation-level-2-city-guilds-2365-02/)

### 18th Edition Course - C&G 2382-26

This essential five-day course delivers a comprehensive breakdown of the IET Wiring Regulations, fully covering the brand-new Amendment 4 (BS 7671). Perfect for practicing electricians and those entering the trade.

[Learn More](https://elec.training/courses/18th-edition-course-city-guilds-2382-26/)

### Stage 2 - Level 3 Diploma C&G 2366-03

Take the next step in your training with the new Level 3 Diploma in Electrical Installations. This progressive stage advances your expertise into critical industry skills, including electrical design, advanced science, environmental systems, inspection and testing, and fault-finding.

[Learn More](https://elec.training/courses/electrical-installation-level-3-2366/)

### Stage 3 - NVQ Level 3 C&G 2357

The electrical NVQ is structured to make completing it as simple as possible. The C&G 2357 NVQ can be completed remotely via our online system, enabling you to upload various types of evidence and also complete numerous assessments as you study, rather than the 2-3 site visits that many traditional providers offer.

[Learn More](https://elec.training/courses/nvq-level-3-electrical-installation-maintenance-city-guilds-2357/)

### Stage 4 - AM2

Upon successful completion of your portfolio you will be required to undertake an AM2 test. This is c onducted at an independent centre a total of 2.5 days at a fee of circa £800.00. \*AM2 assessment is not included. We arrange this with you as per due process at a AM2 assessment centre local to you.

[Click here](https://elec.training/electricians-career-map/#)

### Stage 5 - Apply for NVQ

Having completed your AM2 assessment and providing us with your certificate, we will claim your NVQ. Allowing you to move to the final step.
