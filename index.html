Your Route

Five stages to your
ECS Gold Card

Most people take one to three years end to end, depending on your schedule and how quickly you gather your NVQ evidence.

Start at stage one
1

Level 2 Diploma

Your starting point. Circuits, tools, safe working and the regulations, taught in the workshop rather than from a book. Most people also sit the 18th Edition around here.

C&G 2365-02
2

Level 3 Diploma

Electrical design, advanced science, environmental systems, inspection and testing, and fault finding. This is where you stop being a beginner.

C&G 2366-03
3

NVQ Level 3 on site

Real work, recorded as evidence. We place you with an employer, you upload evidence through our online system, and an assessor visits you two or three times.

C&G 2357
4

AM2 assessment

Two and a half days at an independent centre, testing that you can actually do the job under exam conditions. We prepare you for it before you go.

Independent centre
5

Claim your Gold Card

We claim your NVQ once your AM2 certificate is in. You then apply to the JIB for the ECS Gold Card and you can work domestic, commercial and industrial.

FAQ

The questions we get
asked every week

Most adult learners begin with the City and Guilds 2365 Level 2 Diploma before progressing to the 2366 Level 3 Diploma. You then complete a work based Level 3 NVQ, gather evidence from real electrical work and pass the AM2 assessment. You will also need a current understanding of the BS 7671 Wiring Regulations. Once that is done you may be eligible to apply for an ECS Installation Electrician Gold Card.

The City and Guilds 2365 Level 2 Diploma. It is designed for people who know nothing about the trade, covering circuits, tools and regulations from the beginning. Hands on workshops simulate real installations so you build confidence, and the scheduling suits adults balancing work or family. It leads straight into Level 3.

The full journey usually takes one to three years, depending on your course schedule, your access to suitable electrical work and how quickly you complete your NVQ workplace evidence. The NVQ stage alone commonly takes six to eighteen months, though timescales vary from person to person.

Level 2 and Level 3 are £3,249 each including VAT, the NVQ 2357 is £2,249 including VAT with site assessments charged separately, and the 18th Edition is £599 including VAT. Payment plans may be available to spread the cost, subject to eligibility and the applicable terms. Call a course adviser for current package prices.

Yes. You cannot finish the NVQ without real site evidence, so we guarantee a work placement for every NVQ learner. We have placed over 100 people with employers through our network. Your assessor supports you on documenting the evidence, and it is uploaded through our online system rather than waiting on site visits.

Yes. The City and Guilds Diplomas and the NVQ 2357 are nationally accredited across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, and recognised by employers and the JIB. They align with BS 7671 and the Electrotechnical Certification Scheme, and they lead to the ECS cards you need for site access.

The modular structure allows part time or intensive study to fit around your life, prior experience in related trades can speed you up, and mentoring covers the gaps.
