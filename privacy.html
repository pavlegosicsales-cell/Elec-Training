Privacy policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Who we are

Elec Training is a UK electrical training provider and a City and Guilds Approved Centre, centre number 012036. Our head office is Bank House, 8 Cherry Street, Birmingham, B2 5AL. We are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office under registration number ZB585089.

This policy explains what we do with the personal details you give us through this website. If anything here is unclear, email enquiry@elec.training and we will explain it.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through a form on this site, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • The course you are asking about, your current stage of training, when you want to start, and roughly where you are based
  • Anything you choose to write in the message box

We do not ask for payment details, identity documents or any special category data through this website. Please do not send them through the enquiry form.

Why we collect it and our lawful basis

We use your details to answer your enquiry: to call or email you back about course dates, prices, finance options and the right route to qualification for your situation. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests, specifically responding to someone who has asked us for information about our courses.

We will not add you to a marketing list from an enquiry alone. If we ever want to send you marketing, we will ask you separately and you can say no.

Who sees your details

Your enquiry is seen by the Elec Training staff who handle course enquiries. We do not sell your personal details, and we do not share them with third parties for their own marketing.

We use ordinary business software to receive and store enquiries, such as email and form handling services. Those providers process the data on our instructions and are not permitted to use it for anything else. Where a provider stores data outside the UK, we rely on the safeguards that provider has in place for international transfers.

If you go on to enrol on a course, your details will also be used to register you with the relevant awarding organisation, such as City and Guilds. We will tell you about this at the point of enrolment.

How long we keep it

If you enquire and do not enrol, we keep your enquiry for up to 24 months so we can pick up the conversation if you come back to us, then delete it. If you enrol, learner records are kept for as long as the awarding organisation and our own obligations require.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • Correct anything that is wrong or incomplete
  • Delete your details
  • Restrict or object to how we use them
  • Provide your data in a portable format

To do any of these, email enquiry@elec.training or call 0330 822 5337. We will respond within one month. There is no charge.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. We would rather you came to us first so we can put it right.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics or embedded content in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Links to other sites

This site links out to third party sites including Trustpilot, Google, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube. Once you follow those links you are on their site and their privacy policy applies, not ours.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. This version was published on 1 September 2026.
